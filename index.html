01 — PROFILE
Two decades of keeping networks talking

Beverlyne is a results-driven telecom engineer with over 15 years across Radio Access Network (RAN), IPRAN, rollout, integration and optimisation on multi-vendor 2G, 3G, 4G/LTE and emerging 5G networks. Her work spans Ericsson and Nokia radio technologies, network modernisation, KPI analysis, troubleshooting and performance improvement for operators including MTN, Airtel, Orange and Movicel.

She is skilled in radio performance analysis, root-cause investigation, site acceptance, parameter optimisation and customer-facing technical support — with a track record of improving coverage, capacity and operational efficiency on large-scale telecom projects. She reports and monitors performance using Power BI, Excel and operational dashboards to support strategic decision-making.

Alongside her field engineering career, she is completing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, focused on data analysis, databases and automation.
